# Past Projects

A working record of older projects that are useful context but do not necessarily belong on the resume. Some of these may become homepage content later.

## Kickstarter Campaign Microsites

As a freelance developer at Upwork, I implemented responsive designs for two product microsites promoting MYLO Kickstarter campaigns. Both campaigns were successfully funded.

Keywords: JavaScript, SASS, Responsive Design

### A Watch by You

**April 2014**

The microsite was built to gather user feedback for an upcoming Kickstarter campaign. The feedback helped shape the final product: a set of watches with multiple variations.

The project was designed to work across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. The campaign was successfully funded.

- [Original microsite](http://designawatch.okmylo.com/)
- [Kickstarter campaign](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/mijlo/everyday-watch-1-timepiece-10-variations-365-days/creator)

### MYLO: Essentials x A Better Backpack

**November 2013**

The microsite showcased essential items for designers and creatives, with a responsive layout built to work across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. The campaign was successfully funded.

- [Original microsite](http://essentials.okmylo.com/)
- [Kickstarter campaign](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/mijlo/a-better-backpack-sustainable-design-sustainable-f)
